Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Short version: Yummy Loop does not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account. Your coins, level progress, boosters and picture gallery stay on your own device. We never see them. The only data leaving your phone is what Google AdMob needs in order to show ads.

What the app stores

Saved on your device only, never sent anywhere:

  • The level you are on, your coins and boosters, and your win streak and treat-chest progress
  • The pictures you have eaten (your gallery) and the chapters you have finished
  • Which tutorials and "new feature" cards you have already seen
  • The date of your last daily treat and your streak, and how many free-coin videos you watched today
  • Sound effects, music and vibration on/off, and your chosen language

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. There is no server and no backup — if you uninstall, the progress is gone for good.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. To do that, Google may collect and process:

  • An advertising identifier and app set ID (resettable IDs, not your name)
  • Your IP address, used to estimate your approximate location
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • Diagnostic and device information, such as device type, operating system version, app launch time and hang rate

Google uses this data to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud, and it is sent over encrypted connections.

We configure AdMob with a maximum ad content rating of G (suitable for everyone). Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose to watch one to get an extra bench and keep playing, double the coins after a level, get a free booster, get free coins in the shop, or double the daily treat. Google's own privacy policy explains what they do with this data: https://policies.google.com/privacy — you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time under Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

Users in Europe

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app first asks for your consent (using Google's consent form) before your data is used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. If you do not consent, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

Data security

Yummy Loop has no server and no account, so we never receive or store your data anywhere. Your progress is kept in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. The advertising data described above is collected and sent by Google's AdMob SDK over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and handled under Google's own privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

Your progress stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings; either one deletes it immediately. We hold no copy that would need deleting.

Children

All content is rated G. The game is not directed to children under 13; its target audience on Google Play is 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Permissions

The app requests only the permissions Google AdMob needs to show ads (such as internet access, network state and the advertising ID), plus vibration. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, files or location.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with an updated date at the top.
